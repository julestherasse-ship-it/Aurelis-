About

Who is Mr Gaston?

Mr Gaston is an artisan friterie in Mons (Chaussée de Binche 141). Since 2018 it has served fries, burgers and snacks made with local products — dine-in, takeaway or online order.

Site goals

What the website needed to deliver

Highlight the craft identity, present the menu clearly, make online ordering easy, and keep address, hours and contact instantly reachable — on desktop and mobile.

Art direction

Deep black, gold and product photography

A dark premium atmosphere, expressive typography, focus on the signature “Le Poivré” burger, and simple conversion-led navigation (menu, order, contact).

Features

What was actually built

Based on the delivered site — no invented commercial results.

  • Signature heroFeatured “Le Poivré” burger with price, description and clear CTAs.
  • MenuStructured presentation of burgers, snacks, fries, sauces and formats.
  • Online orderingLink to the external Foodbooking order flow.
  • GalleryVisual gallery with lightbox for product photography.
  • LocationAddress, today’s hours, map and directions.
  • TrustHouse / services / reviews sections, FAQ and final CTA.
  • TechnicalResponsive Next.js site, restaurant JSON-LD SEO, optional analytics.
Mobile preview

Built for the phone

Most guests look up an address or place an order from a smartphone. The mobile path keeps the hero, menu and Order / View menu CTAs front and centre.
